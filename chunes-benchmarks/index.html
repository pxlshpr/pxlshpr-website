Leaderboard (tap a column to sort)

Quality = blind-judge musical correctness (0–10, Opus-independent). gJac = genre agreement with Opus. Track-1 parametric (no web).
Key findings
  • A cheaper model beats our Opus. gemini-3-flash scored 8.56/10 blind vs Opus 8.22 — won 5 of 9, never worst.
  • Opus makes real errors. It tagged a 1956 Stan Getz swing standard “Smooth Jazz” (a 1980s genre); gemini said “Jazz + Swing”. Opus was rated worst on 2 of 9.
  • deepseek-v4-flash is the value pick — $0.17/1k, fast, 2nd on agreement; its misses are mood-nuance + the odd obscure genre slip.
  • Web is the great equalizer on obscure artists (+0.07 to +0.33 genre Jaccard). It turned a wrong “Drum & Bass” call on hardcore band Speed into a correct 10/10 — but is higher-variance.
  • Reasoning hurts here. qwen-thinking was slowest (46s), priciest ($3.96/1k), and lowest-quality of the cheap models. Pin reasoning off.
